# Changelog

## 1.1.1

- **Fixed:** With Way Back Pelican Town (and other retextures that redraw buildings for the current season only),
  buildings, fences and trees no longer turn half into the plain game's look during a transition, especially with
  a recolour installed. They now keep their look until the season changes, while the ground around them still
  changes gradually.
- **Fixed:** Small bits of the game's own roofs no longer show through snowy roofs from those retextures.

## 1.1.0

- **New:** Works with Seasons of Stardew (detected automatically). Seasons now blend at the end of the third month,
  and snow days are only added in winter.
- **New:** Works with expansion mods like SVE, Ridgeside Village, Sunberry Village and East Scarp. Their maps
  change gradually too.
- **New:** Art another mod shows only in the current season (like a seasonal special order board) is no longer
  painted over.
- **Improved:** Stone paths and town squares no longer melt as squares.
- **Improved:** Snow drifts against trees, fences and cliffs now melt in soft patches.
- **Improved:** Snow and ice from mods like Elle's Seasonal Vanilla Buildings and SVE now melt gradually too.
- **Improved:** Buildings another mod already makes snowy don't get extra roof snow.
- **Improved:** Falling snow, leaves and petals follow each area's own weather.
- **Fixed:** Straight edges where another mod edits a map (like Beach Minecart).
- **Fixed:** Thin dark lines on sand and dirt.
- **Fixed:** Flowers and grass growing on beach sand and paths.
- **Fixed:** White squares on the town fountain with Elle's Seasonal Vanilla Buildings.
- **Fixed:** Another mod's old art sometimes showing for the rest of the day after it changed.
- **Fixed:** Parts of the change missing from whole-map screenshots of big maps.
